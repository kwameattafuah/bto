Who We Are

More than basketball.

Beat the Odds Basketball Foundation is committed to using basketball as a platform for youth development. Our programmes are designed not only to build athletic ability, but to instil discipline, confidence, leadership and strong character in every young participant.

Through structured engagement, we aim to contribute meaningfully to the social and personal development of young people within our community — on the court and well beyond it.

Whatever the odds, we show up, we put in the work, and we rise together.

Discipline

Showing up, putting in the reps, and holding ourselves to a high standard every session.

Development

Real, measurable growth — skills, fitness and mindset that compound over the season.

Resilience

Pushing through setbacks on the court and carrying that strength into life.

For Parents

What to expect.

Everything you need to know before enrolling your child.

📅

Season Structure

A full season runs three months (30th May – 30th August 2026), with flexible schedule options to suit your family — from twice a week to pay-as-you-go.

💰

Session Options

Twice a week — GH₵ 1,500 / season. Saturdays only — GH₵ 1,000 / season. Free flow, pay per session — GH₵ 150. A one-time GH₵ 250 registration fee applies on enrolment.

🧾

Where Fees Go

As a non-profit, every cedi goes straight into facilities, equipment, coaching, safety arrangements and day-to-day running. No funds are taken for personal or commercial gain.

🛡

Safety First

Sessions are supervised, with a medical professional or basic medical support and an emergency response plan in place. We collect secondary contacts, authorised pickup details and medical information at registration.

💪

What Your Child Gains

Beyond basketball skills — discipline, confidence, leadership, fitness and strong character, in a positive and encouraging community.

🏣

What to Bring

Just sportswear, trainers and a reusable water bottle — we provide a water dispenser to keep it topped up. All equipment is provided, including basketballs sized for every age group we train.

Questions

Good to know.

How do I enrol my child?

Fill out our online signup form via any “Register” button on this page. You’ll provide parent and athlete details, choose a session option, share emergency contacts and any medical information, and complete the consent sections. We’ll then be in touch with everything you need for the first session.

How is the programme structured?

Participation is organised as a structured training season running three months, from 30th May to 30th August 2026. Depending on the schedule chosen, that’s either two training days a week (up to 24 sessions) or one day a week (12 sessions). Fees are tied to the full seasonal structure and cover participation across the complete programme cycle.

What are the fees and what do they cover?

Choose the option that suits your family: twice a week (GH₵ 1,500/season), Saturdays only (GH₵ 1,000/season), or free flow pay-per-session (GH₵ 150). A one-time GH₵ 250 registration fee applies on enrolment. As a non-profit, all funds are used strictly for facilities, equipment, coaching, safety arrangements and the day-to-day running of the programme — never for personal or commercial gain.

How do you keep my child safe?

Sessions are supervised, with a medical professional or basic medical support and an emergency response plan in place. At registration we collect a secondary contact, an authorised pickup person, and any medical information, allergies or concerns so coaches are fully informed. Photo and media consent is requested separately and is entirely optional — it never affects your child’s participation.

Where and when are sessions held?

Sessions run every Saturday (10:00–11:30am) and Sunday (4:00–5:00pm) at Earls Court Apartments, Agostinho Neto Close, Accra, throughout the season. Extra Thursday sessions are available on request.

How can I volunteer or support BTO?

We welcome volunteer coaches, partners and supporters. Reach out at btobasketballfoundation@gmail.com and we’ll tell you how you can get involved.
